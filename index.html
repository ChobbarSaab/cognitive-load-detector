AI Cognitive Load Detector

Students take a short learning task here. The site times it, counts changed answers, asks how hard it felt, and the AI estimates their cognitive load as Low, Moderate or High.

Take the task Try the model

A short learning task (about 10–15 minutes)

  • 10 multiple-choice questions that get harder as you go.
  • Work without a calculator. Rough paper is fine.
  • The timer starts when you press Start and stops when you submit.
  • Afterwards you rate how difficult the task was and how much effort it took.

This is not a test of you. Your name is seen only by the person running the project and is never shown in the results. Answers are used only for this school project.
